Popular Tamil Cinema

Discover the highlights of some of the most beloved films.

Mersal (2017)

Genre: Action, Thriller
  • Global Blockbuster hit with overwhelming fan love.
  • Stunning performance by Vijay in a triple role.
  • Captivating musical score by A.R. Rahman.
  • Powerful social message addressing medical corruption.
  • Crossed over ₹250 crore worldwide.

Naai Sekar (2022)

Genre: Comedy, Family
  • A perfect fun-filled family entertainer.
  • Unique and imaginative plot: A man swaps souls with a dog!
  • Light-hearted comedy that appeals to all age groups.
  • Brilliant comic timing and amusing sequences.

Naai Sekar Returns (2022)

Genre: Comedy, Entertainer
  • Long-awaited comeback of the legendary comedian Vadivelu.
  • Hilarious adventures and nostalgic comic moments.
  • Lively music that complements the high-energy entertainment.
  • A treat for fans of classic Tamil comedy.

The Legend (2022)

Genre: Action, Drama
  • Action-packed commercial entertainer with massive scale.
  • Strong ensemble cast bringing a diverse dynamic to the screen.
  • Energetic and catchy music by Harris Jayaraj.
  • A larger-than-life cinematic experience for action lovers.
